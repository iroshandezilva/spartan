Semantic Color has four modes: Light, Dark, Light High Contrast, and Dark High Contrast. Primary colors also depend on the active Primary hue (Blue, Purple, Orange, or Sky). This site's header toggle switches Light and Dark; see [Theming](/docs/theming) for the others.

## Guidance

- Pair a fill with its matching text token, for example `--sp-color-primary-default` with `--sp-color-primary-foreground`.
- Do not read primitive color variables (`--sp-prim-color-*`) in components. Semantic tokens carry the mode and hue logic.
- Focus rings use `--sp-color-focus-ring-default`.
